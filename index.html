Our Story.txt
Made in the in-between.

Business Casual began during the hardest season of my life.

FOUNDER PHOTO
COMING SOON
the hands behind every piece

I moved home to care for my mom after she was diagnosed with ALS. As she needed full-time care, traditional work stopped fitting into our life.

I started making jewelry because it was something I could pick up between caregiving moments and put down the second she needed me. There was no perfect schedule. There were only little pockets of time and whatever I could make inside them.

During that same season, I realized how badly I needed space from my phone. I loved the idea behind physical app blockers, learned how NFC tags worked, and figured out how to place them inside the jewelry I was already making.

Business Casual came from combining those two needs: making something expressive with my hands and finding a physical way back to the life happening around me.

Every purchase supports me as an independent maker and caregiver while my mom is living with ALS. As the business grows, my goal is to establish a transparent annual contribution to ALS research in her honor.

What the jewelry can and cannot do

An NFC tag is a trigger, not a blocker by itself. A website cannot block other apps. True app blocking requires compatible software and the phone owner’s permission. The first drop will include setup guidance, and we will never hide that distinction.
