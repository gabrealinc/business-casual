Create a Custom Shortcut

MAKE THIS PIECE YOURS

One tap. Your Focus. Your music.

Apple Shortcuts can recognize your piece and run the actions you choose whenever you tap it.

Set up your custom shortcut

  1. Open Apple’s Shortcuts app and tap Automation.
  2. Create a new Personal Automation and choose NFC.
  3. Scan this piece and name it “Business Casual.”
  4. Add Set Focus, Play Music, or both.
  5. Choose Run Immediately, then save and test your tap.

Your Business Casual link can stay on the tag. Shortcuts recognizes the physical piece itself.

Focus silences notifications, but it does not prevent you from opening apps.

Why This Exists.txt

Made in the in-between.

Business Casual began during the hardest season of my life.

I moved home to care for my mom after she was diagnosed with ALS. As she needed full-time care, traditional work stopped fitting into our life.

I started making jewelry because I could pick it up between caregiving moments and put it down the second she needed me. During that same season, I realized how badly I needed space from my phone.

Business Casual came from combining those two needs: making something expressive with my hands and finding a physical way back to the life happening around me.

This piece is not here to tell you how to live. It is a small invitation to choose.

Made by hand to bring you back to your life. ♡

TAPPING YOUR PIECE

  1. Unlock your phone and keep the screen awake.
  2. Hold the NFC area near the top back of the phone for a few seconds.
  3. Tap the notification that appears.

Thick or magnetic cases and nearby metal can interfere. Try removing the case or moving the piece slightly.

Nothing here changes your phone without your permission. Focus modes and blocking apps require their own one-time setup.
